Title: Laptop Running Slow

Problem: User reports their laptop is slow, freezing, or taking long to open applications.

Steps to resolve:
1. Restart the laptop (resolves most temporary slowdowns).
2. Check Task Manager for high CPU/memory usage applications and close unnecessary ones.
3. Clear temporary files and browser cache.
4. Check available disk space — free up space if storage is nearly full.
5. Ensure the laptop has the latest OS and antivirus updates installed.

Escalation: If performance issues persist after these steps, escalate to Hardware Support to check for possible disk or RAM issues.
