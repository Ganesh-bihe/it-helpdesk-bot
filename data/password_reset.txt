Title: How to Reset Your Windows Password

Problem: User is locked out of their Windows account or forgot their password.

Steps to resolve:
1. On the login screen, click "Forgot Password" or "Reset Password".
2. Enter your registered email address or security PIN.
3. Follow the verification steps sent to your email or phone.
4. Create a new password following the company policy (minimum 8 characters, one number, one special character).
5. Log in with the new password to confirm it works.

Escalation: If the user does not receive a verification email/OTP after 10 minutes, or the account remains locked after reset,
 escalate to Level 2 IT Support with the user's employee ID and last successful login date.
