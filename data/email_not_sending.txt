Title: Unable to Send Emails

Problem: User can receive emails but cannot send them; emails get stuck in the Outbox.

Steps to resolve:
1. Check internet connectivity.
2. Confirm the email isn't oversized (large attachments can block sending) — try removing attachments and resending.
3. Check if Outlook is in "Work Offline" mode and disable it.
4. Restart Outlook.
5. Check the Outbox for stuck emails and try resending manually.

Escalation: If emails remain stuck after these steps, escalate to Email/Exchange Support with a screenshot of the error message.
