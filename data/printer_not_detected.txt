Title: Printer Not Detected / Not Printing

Problem: User's computer cannot find or connect to the office printer.

Steps to resolve:
1. Confirm the printer is powered on and connected to the network/Wi-Fi.
2. On the user's PC, go to Settings > Printers & Scanners and check if the printer is listed.
3. If not listed, click "Add Printer" and search again.
4. Restart the print spooler service (Services app > Print Spooler > Restart).
5. Try printing a test page.

Escalation: If the printer still isn't detected, escalate to Hardware Support with the printer model and location.
