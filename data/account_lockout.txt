Title: User Account Locked Out

Problem: User's account is locked after multiple failed login attempts.

Steps to resolve:
1. Confirm the user's identity (employee ID, registered email).
2. Check the account status in Active Directory / IAM portal.
3. Unlock the account and prompt the user to reset their password on next login.
4. Advise the user to update saved passwords on other devices to avoid repeat lockouts.

Escalation: If the account locks again within 24 hours, escalate to Security Team to check for suspicious login attempts.
