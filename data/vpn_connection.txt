Title: VPN Connection Issues

Problem: User is unable to connect to the company VPN, or the connection keeps dropping.

Steps to resolve:
1. Confirm the user has an active internet connection outside the VPN.
2. Restart the VPN client application.
3. Re-enter VPN credentials (username and password) — check for caps lock or typos.
4. Ensure the VPN client is updated to the latest version.
5. Restart the laptop and try reconnecting.

Escalation: If the VPN still fails to connect after these steps, escalate to Network Support with the user's device name and error code shown (if any).
