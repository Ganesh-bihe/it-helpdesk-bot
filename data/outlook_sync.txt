Title: Outlook Not Syncing / Emails Not Receiving

Problem: User's Outlook is not receiving new emails or shows a "disconnected" status.

Steps to resolve:
1. Check the internet connection is active.
2. In Outlook, go to Send/Receive tab and click "Update Folder".
3. Check if Outlook is in "Work Offline" mode — click to disable it if so.
4. Restart Outlook completely.
5. If issue persists, remove and re-add the email account under File > Account Settings.

Escalation: If sync fails after account re-add, escalate to Email/Exchange Support with the user's email address and error message.
